Our Heritage

About Taste of Bali

Preserving ancestral Balinese recipes and the Tri Hita Karana philosophy in Ubud.

Generations of Balinese Taste

Founded by a native family deeply rooted in Ubud's cultural community, Taste of Bali began with a simple mission: to preserve and share the intricate legacy of local Balinese food.

In 2018, we opened our tropical bamboo doors to curious travelers. To us, cooking is about community, spirituality, and our ancestral "Tri Hita Karana" philosophy of living in harmony with nature and humanity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Tap any question to view details

Is hotel transport included in Ubud?+
Yes! Complimentary round-trip hotel shuttle is included for all bookings within central Ubud. Pickups from Canggu, Seminyak, or Nusa Dua can be arranged for a small surcharge.
Are vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options available?+
Absolutely. All Balinese dishes we cook can be customized to support vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, nut-free, and halal dietary needs.
What is your cancellation policy?+
We offer completely free cancellation up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled class.
What should I wear to the cooking class?+
We recommend light, comfortable tropical clothing and closed-toe walking shoes for the morning market tour.
