Our Signature Dishes

Discover the authentic flavors of traditional Balinese cuisine

Bebek Betutu

Slow-cooked duck infused with traditional Balinese spices, herbs, and rich aromatic flavors.

Jaje Bali

Traditional Balinese sweets made with rice flour, sticky rice, coconut, and palm sugar.

Sambal Matah

A fresh Balinese raw sambal combining shallots, lemongrass, chilies, lime, and aromatic coconut oil.

Nasi Goreng

Indonesian fried rice tossed with fragrant spices and savory seasonings for a comforting, flavorful meal.

Sate Lilit

Seasoned minced fish or meat blended with Balinese spices and wrapped around lemongrass or bamboo skewers.

Lawar

A traditional Balinese mixture of finely chopped vegetables, grated coconut, herbs, and rich local spices.
